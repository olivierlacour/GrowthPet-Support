Privacy Policy

Our approach

GrowthPet is designed to keep your pet information private. We do not sell your data, and the app does not require an account to use its core features.

Information you provide

The information you enter in GrowthPet—such as pet profiles, measurements, photos and notes—is stored locally on your device. This data is used only to provide the app’s features.

Backups and exports

When you create a backup or export a report, you choose where to save or share that file. Once you share it with another app or service, its handling is governed by that provider’s privacy policy.

Analytics and tracking

GrowthPet does not use advertising trackers and does not collect personal information for targeted advertising.

Children’s privacy

GrowthPet is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact
